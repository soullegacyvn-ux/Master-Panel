MASTER PANEL 3.2.1

UPGRADE: Settings > Updates > Check > Download > Update & restart.
From 3.0.1 the app restarts into a compatibility stage, then completes the matching
panel/widget package. Close games and leave the panel hidden while it finishes.
A Windows installation progress window may appear. Settings are preserved.

FRESH INSTALL / MANUAL FALLBACK:
1. Exit the old app from its tray menu.
2. Open Master-Panel-v3.2.1-Setup.exe and approve Windows' Administrator prompt.
3. Click Install. The default folder is C:\Program Files\Master Panel.
4. Open Master Panel from Start or the desktop shortcut.

Setup installs the desktop app and Xbox Game Bar widget together. It does not
require opening a command window. The widget uses its included self-signed
certificate and a local loopback connection. Saved user settings are retained.

DESKTOP: press the physical Keyboard/Mouse <-> Gamepad Swap button.
WIDGET: Win+G > Widgets > Master Panel. LT/RT switch widget tabs.
Menu opens Settings / header actions; B returns to the current menu.
Release held inputs after opening or switching focus.

Controller mapping in the widget uses an editor inside the widget content.
Choose a source, choose its output, press Done, then Apply/Flash. Done only
stages the mapping. Permanent flash still needs review and backup/readback.

The power chart shares the last 60 one-second battery samples between both
views. Its size follows the panel and its W scale follows the readings. Missing
samples are shown as gaps. It creates no extra sensor polling worker.

Fan output shows the software manual range, measured RPM and thermal status.
Auto duty is unavailable when the EC reports only automatic mode. The app does
not invent an RPM minimum/maximum or guarantee a safe temperature from RPM.

WebView2 is required for the full UI, PawnIO for supported hardware controls,
and Xbox Game Bar for the widget. Open with Windows restores saved controls at
sign-in. No BIOS or battery charge-limit writes are added.

Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Master Panel.
This removes the app, widget and its own certificate; user settings remain.

Hardware controls and real Game Bar controller focus need Win 5 testing.

VIBRATION: Controller > Vibration level > Off / Low / High. Temporary applies
for the current session; Restore reverts temporary controller changes. To keep
it, select Permanent > Flash vibration > confirm. Do not disconnect during a
write. Physical vibration and reboot persistence require a Win 5 check.

COMPONENTS: Settings > Components > Download > Install. The app verifies each
publisher download and opens its normal Windows installer. Finish that wizard,
then check the connection. PawnIO may need a Master Panel restart. Nothing is
silently installed. An installed FxSound starts in its tray on widget activation.


TDP RANGE AND RESET
Performance > TDP range: enter Battery max and DC max, then Save range.
This changes slider limits only. Select a TDP explicitly to apply it.
Caution appears above 65 W on battery or above 75 W on DC.
Reset selects 45 W with boost off, or your lower active source ceiling.
Plugging in does not automatically increase TDP. Firmware may enforce lower limits.
